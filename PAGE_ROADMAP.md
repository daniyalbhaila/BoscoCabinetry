## **Implementation Strategy**

### **ROI-Based Approach:**

1. **Start with Tier 1** - These 8 pages will drive 80% of your organic revenue
2. **Add Tier 2** selectively - Focus on highest commercial value first
3. **Build Tier 3** for AEO dominance and trust signals
4. **Consider Tier 4-5** only after core pages are performing

### **Key Insights:**

- **"Kitchen"** keywords dominate search volume (8,100+ monthly)
- **Service+Location combos** are the highest converting "money pages"
- **"Near Me" searches** are exploding (3,300+ monthly for kitchen renovations)
- **AEO opportunity** with FAQ and cost guides can capture voice search
- **Comparison pages** intercept competitor research traffic

### **Commercial Value Legend:**

- **$$$$$** = $10K+ average project value, high search volume
- **$$$$** = $5-10K average project value, good volume
- **$$$** = $2-5K average project value, medium volume
- **$$** = <$2K average project value, lower volume

**Focus on Tier 1 first - these 8 pages could drive 80% of your organic lead generation and revenue!**
