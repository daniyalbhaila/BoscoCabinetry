## Project Overview

This Mississauga renovation was driven by deeply personal circumstances. After years of renting out their property, the homeowners decided to move back in. With the husband relocating for medical treatment, the renovation took on special significance - creating spaces that would provide comfort, beauty, and peace of mind during a challenging chapter.

The project encompassed refreshing the kitchen and transforming two upstairs bathrooms into serene retreats designed for lasting comfort and care.

## The Design Vision

The couple wanted spaces that felt both luxurious and deeply personal - not ostentatious, but genuinely comforting. The design needed to support daily routines while providing the calm, peaceful atmosphere essential during medical treatment and recovery.

## Elegant Bathroom Design

The two upstairs bathrooms feature elegant marble-inspired tile that brings natural beauty and sophistication. The material choice provides the refined aesthetic of natural stone while offering practical benefits - durability, easy maintenance, and consistent quality throughout.

Warm gold accents throughout add refined luxury without feeling cold or clinical. These touches create warmth and richness, making the bathrooms feel genuinely welcoming rather than merely functional.

## Ambient Lighting

Carefully designed ambient lighting elevates the entire atmosphere. The lighting isn't harsh or institutional - it's soft, adjustable, and creates the serene mood essential for a space meant to provide comfort and respite.

## Kitchen Harmony

The kitchen renovation complemented the bathroom work, featuring the same clean, timeless aesthetic. Together, these spaces create a unified home that feels calm and contemporary - a complete retreat designed for the couple's needs.

## The Result

The finished home ties together beautifully - bathrooms and kitchen all sharing the same peaceful, refined aesthetic. This calm, contemporary retreat demonstrates how thoughtful design can create spaces that support wellbeing during life's most challenging moments, providing lasting comfort and care through beautiful, functional design.
